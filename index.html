Waiting for a photo

When you check a photo, the answer appears here. SafeSnap will never tell you a photo is safe. It can only say what it found, and what it might have missed.

How it works

  1. Your device looks first

    A small AI model in Chrome checks the photo on this computer. The photo does not leave your device for this step.

  2. You decide

    Keep the photo private, retake or crop it, or ask for a longer description. Nothing happens without your choice.

  3. The cloud only if you say yes

    A more detailed description needs a cloud model. SafeSnap asks before each photo, and always shows which engine ran.
